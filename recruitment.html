Join CVVD Lab

We are always looking for highly motivated students and postdocs who are passionate about computer vision, machine learning, and their applications in robotics and industrial settings.

Ph.D. Students

We have multiple funded Ph.D. positions available starting next Fall. We are particularly interested in candidates with strong backgrounds in math, computer science, and prior research experience in computer vision.

How to apply: Please email your CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of research interests to contact@cvvd-lab.edu.

Master's / Undergraduates

If you are currently enrolled and looking for a research project or thesis topic in quality inspection, semantic segmentation, or spatial measurement, we have several exciting projects available.

How to apply: Reach out directly to the lab director or any of the current Ph.D. students.
