Join CVVD Lab

We are always looking for highly motivated students and postdocs who are passionate about computer vision, machine learning, and their applications in robotics and industrial settings.

Ph.D. Students

We have multiple funded Ph.D. positions for eligible candidates. We welcome backgrounds in any engineering discipline or computer science — what matters is the appetite for the problem, not the label on your degree.

How to apply: Please email your CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of research interests to contact@cvvdlab.org.

Master's / Undergraduates

If you are currently enrolled and looking for a research project or thesis topic in quality inspection, semantic segmentation, or spatial measurement, we have several exciting projects available.

How to apply: Reach out directly to the lab principal, Dr. Zulhilmi, or to any of the current Ph.D. students.
